# BuildAid

**Reference images over your screen while you build — with adjustable opacity, 3D geometric guides, and synchronized listening channels.**

Stop alt-tabbing to look at your reference. BuildAid pins images inside the game on floating panels you can move, resize, zoom and fade, so the reference sits right where you're building. It also brings 3D in-world holograms, geometric block guides (spheres, domes, roofs), a Litematica-style area selection, a block-aligned grid, a compact info HUD, and Discord-style synchronized music rooms to listen together with friends in any server!

Everything is done from **one menu**, and only **four keys** are bound out of the box.

Fully client-side: works in singleplayer and on any server without needing any server mods.

---

## Features

### 🖼️ Reference Image Panels
- Floating panels you **drag, resize, zoom and pan** with the mouse without pausing the game.
- **Opacity slider** — turn references into translucent overlays over your build.
- **Fullscreen ghost overlay** for pixel art, facades, and large murals.
- Three ways to add images: **paste from clipboard (`Ctrl+V`)**, **open file**, or **paste link**.
- Integrated gallery with automatic thumbnails that saves everything across sessions.

### 🎵 Synchronized Music & Channels
- **Listen together with friends** in Discord-style voice/music channels.
- **Automatic server detection**: joins the channel for your current Minecraft server automatically!
- Supports **YouTube videos/lives**, web radios, and direct audio stream links.
- Sleek **in-game Mini Player HUD** with progress bar, volume controls, and track skip.

### 🌐 3D In-World Holograms
- Project images directly into the 3D block world at real block scale.
- Lock aspect ratios and orient towards North, South, East, West, or flat on the ground.

### 📐 3D Geometric Shape Guides
Visual block blueprints with material counts and layer-by-layer (Y) previews:
- Box, Cylinder, Sphere, Dome, Pyramid, Cone, Gable Roof, Hip Roof, Spiral Staircase (Helix), Arch / Vault, and Torus (Ring).
- Hollow or solid modes with customizable shell thickness and rotation.

### 📦 Area Selection (Litematica-style)
- Non-intrusive mode with distinct colored corner markers (Corner 1 Blue, Corner 2 Orange).
- Real-time measurements: width × height × depth, total block volume, and diagonal distance.

### 🏁 Building Grid & Info HUD
- Ground grid aligned to blocks with highlighted chunk borders.
- Customizable info HUD: XYZ coordinates, looking direction, biome, light levels, world time, and FPS.

---

## Keys

Only four keys are bound by default to prevent conflicts:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `G` | **Open BuildAid Main Menu** |
| `H` | Show/hide reference panels |
| `B` | Toggle area selection mode |
| `M` | Mark selection corner (looking at a block) |

# BuildAid (Português)

**Imagens de referência sobre a tela enquanto você constrói — com opacidade ajustável, guias geométricos 3D e canais de música sincronizada.**

Chega de dar Alt+Tab para olhar plantas e tutoriais. O BuildAid fixa imagens dentro do jogo em painéis flutuantes que você move, redimensiona e ajusta a transparência em tempo real. Inclui hologramas 3D no mundo, guias geométricos (esferas, cúpulas, telhados), seleção de área estilo Litematica, grade de blocos, HUD de informações e canais de música sincronizada estilo Discord para escutar com amigos em qualquer servidor!

Tudo se faz por **um menu só**, e apenas **quatro teclas** vêm configuradas de fábrica.

Totalmente client-side: funciona em mundos locais e em qualquer servidor sem precisar de nada instalado no servidor.

---

## Funcionalidades

### 🖼️ Painéis de Referência Flutuantes
- Arraste, redimensione e dê zoom nas imagens com o mouse sem pausar o jogo.
- Controle de opacidade com efeito decalque sobre a construção.
- Overlay de tela cheia para alinhar fachadas e pixel art.
- Cole da área de transferência (`Ctrl+V`), abra arquivos ou baixe por URL.

### 🎵 Canais de Música Sincronizada
- Escute músicas sincronizadas com amigos estilo canais do Discord.
- Conecta automaticamente todos os jogadores no mesmo servidor de Minecraft!
- Suporte a links do YouTube, lives e web rádios.
- Mini Player discreto na tela do jogo com barra de progresso.

### 🌐 Hologramas 3D no Mundo
- Projete imagens de referência dentro do mundo de blocos em escala real.

### 📐 Guias Geométricos 3D (11 Formas)
- Esferas, cúpulas, cilindros, pirâmides, cones, telhados, escadas espirais, arcos e anéis com lista de materiais por camada.

### 📦 Seleção de Área (Estilo Litematica)
- Medição precisa de dimensões, volume de blocos e diagonal com cantos coloridos.
